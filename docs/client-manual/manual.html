01How the programme works

Your customers carry a digital stamp card on their phone. There's no app to install and no form to fill in. Your baristas tap a small NFC card on the customer's phone to add a stamp.

The customer journey

1
Tap or scanBarista taps the shop's NFC card, or the customer scans the counter QR
2
Card opensThe card opens in the phone's browser in seconds
3
Collect stampsOne tap per qualifying purchase
4
Claim rewardCustomer shows a 5-minute reward QR
5
RedeemBarista verifies the code and serves the reward

Built-in fraud protection

The system enforces your rules automatically. You don't need to police them at the counter.

  • Cooldown: a minimum time between two stamps for the same customer.
  • Daily cap: the most stamps one customer can collect per day.
  • Tag throttle: stops the same NFC card being tapped repeatedly within a few seconds.
  • Single-use reward codes: each reward QR expires after 5 minutes and works only once, so screenshots can't be reused.

02Signing in

Everyone on your team signs in at the same page, yoursite/staff/login, with their own email address. Please don't share logins: every action is recorded against the person who did it.

First sign-in (new owners and new team members)

  1. Your invitation is created for one exact email address, by your account manager (for owners) or by your shop owner (for team members). Invitations expire after 14 days.
  2. Open yoursite/staff/login and choose the Magic Link tab.
  3. Enter exactly the invited email address, wait for the security check to finish, then tap Send Magic Link.
  4. Open the email and tap the sign-in link. This creates and confirms your login.
  5. The first time, you may see "Access denied: This account does not have active staff permissions." That's normal. Tell your account manager (or your shop owner) that you've signed in, and they'll activate your access.
  6. Once you're activated, sign in again and you'll land on the Operations Console.
ImportantInvitations are not sent by email automatically. After someone is invited, let them know personally and tell them which email address to use.

Everyday sign-in

Open yoursite/staff/login, choose Password (if you've been given one) or Magic Link, wait for the security check, and sign in. On a shared device, tap Sign Out at the top right when you're done.

Working at more than one shop

If you belong to several shops, a Shop drop-down appears at the top of the staff screens. Choose the shop you're serving before you redeem or stamp. Everything you do applies to the selected shop.

03Setting up your shop

Owners and managers set the shop up in the owner console at yoursite/staff/your-slug/settings. The console has six tabs across the top: Rules, Brand, Staff, Tags, Export and Branches.

Your starting settingsEvery new shop starts with: 10 stamps for one reward called "FREE COFFEE", a 30-minute cooldown, a cap of 2 stamps per customer per day, and a 5-second tag throttle. You can change any of these.

Rules tab

Managers can view the rules and preview changes. Only the owner can save them.

SettingAllowed rangeWhat it does
Reward Threshold3–50 stampsStamps needed to earn one reward
Reward LabelUp to 40 charactersThe reward name customers see, for example FREE COFFEE
Cooldown Period0–1440 minutesMinimum time between two stamps for one customer
Daily Cap1–20 stampsMost stamps one customer can collect per day
Tag Anti-Burst Throttle0–60 secondsMinimum gap between two taps on the same NFC card
  1. Change the values you want.
  2. Run the preview. The Customer Impact Preview shows how many existing cards become eligible for a reward straight away, and how many are still collecting.
  3. Save. You'll see "Rules successfully updated." The new rules apply to the next tap.
TipLowering the reward threshold can make many customers eligible at once. Check the preview, and brief your baristas before you save.

Brand tab

  1. Choose a Primary Card Color (the background) and an Accent Color (stamps and text) as colour codes, for example #0A0A0A.
  2. Add a short Shop Tagline.
  3. Upload your Shop Logo and, if you like, Card Art. Use PNG, JPEG or WebP files of 512 KB or less; SVG isn't accepted.
  4. Check the Live Card Preview, then save.

If your two colours are too similar to read easily, the card automatically switches to a readable colour pair.

Branches tab (owner only)

Your shop starts with one branch. Add a branch for each physical location so you can see where each stamp was given.

  1. Under Add New Branch Location, enter a name (for example Ayala Malls Manila Bay) and, optionally, an address.
  2. Use Edit Details to rename a branch, or untick Active to stop stamping there.

All branches share one card by default: the same stamps, rules and limits wherever the customer visits.

Permanent actionGive this branch its own card turns a branch into a separate shop with its own card, rules, team and billing. Customers keep their existing stamps on the main card and start a new card at that branch. This can't be undone. Talk to your account manager first.

Staff tab

  1. Under Invite New Team Member (owner only), enter the person's email address and choose Staff, Manager or Owner, then tap Send Invite.
  2. Tell the person their invitation is ready, and follow First sign-in in chapter 02.
  3. Under Team Members, change someone's role from the drop-down, or tap Deactivate when they leave. Their history is kept, and you can tap Reactivate later.
  4. Each invitation shows as Pending, Accepted or Expired.

04NFC tags & counter QR

An NFC tag is a small card or sticker your barista taps on the customer's phone. Each tag carries one web address that belongs to your shop. Managers and owners create the tags. Allow about five minutes per tag.

What you need

  • NXP NTAG215 cards or stickers. Don't use NTAG213 (too small) or Mifare Classic (iPhones can't read them).
  • A phone with NFC switched on
  • The free NFC Tools app (or NXP TagWriter)
  • An acrylic stand for the counter QR

Good practice

  • Give each tag a clear label, for example Counter Register #1.
  • Always lock the tag after writing it.
  • Keep tags behind the counter, out of customers' reach.
  • Keep one spare tag ready to write.

Step 1: Create the tag

  1. Open the console's Tags tab (yoursite/staff/your-slug/settings/tags).
  2. Under Provision New NFC Tag, type a Tag Label and choose the branch under Assign Branch Location.
  3. Tap create. The new tag appears under its branch.
  4. Tap Copy next to its Production Tap URL.

Step 2: Write the tag

  1. Open NFC Tools and go to Write → Add a record → URL / URI.
  2. Paste the address you copied and tap OK.
  3. Tap Write and hold the tag against the phone: the top of the back on an iPhone, the centre of the back on Android. Hold it still until the phone confirms.

Step 3: Lock the tag

Don't skip thisAnyone with a free app can rewrite an unlocked tag and point it somewhere else.
  • Recommended: NFC Tools → Other → Set Password. You can still rewrite the tag later with the password, so keep the password in a safe place.
  • Permanent: NFC Tools → Other → Lock tag. The tag can never be changed again.

Step 4: Test the tag

  1. Close NFC Tools and tap the tag on a phone.
  2. The shop card should open showing STAMP COLLECTED.
  3. Tap again with the same phone. You should see COOLDOWN ACTIVE, which shows the protection is working.

Replacing a lost, damaged or shared tag

If a tag goes missing, or you think someone has copied its address, replace it at once. This takes about 10 seconds.

  1. In the Tags tab, find the tag and tap Rotate.
  2. The old address stops working immediately, and a new tag with the same label appears.
  3. Write the new address onto a fresh tag (or unlock and rewrite the old one), then lock it.

To stop a tag without replacing it, tap Deactivate.

The counter QR

The counter QR lets first-time customers get their card while they wait. It points to your customer card page, yoursite/s/your-slug.

  • Your account manager can supply print-ready QR artwork. You can also make one yourself with any QR code generator, using your customer card page address.
  • Print the QR at 100% scale, not "fit to page", and place it where customers queue.

05At the counter

These are the everyday tasks for your baristas. Chapter 09 has a one-page version to print and keep at the till.

A new customer

  • Quickest: tap the NFC tag on their phone. A first-timer sees "Claim your first stamp", taps the get-card button, and the first stamp is added automatically.
  • Or: they scan the counter QR and tap GET MY CARD. The card starts with 0 stamps.
  • Customers don't install an app or fill in a form.

Giving a stamp

Hold the tag flat against the phone: the top back edge of an iPhone, or the centre back of an Android phone. If nothing happens on Android, ask the customer to turn on NFC in their quick settings. Then check the banner on their screen:

BannerWhat it means and what to do
STAMP COLLECTEDDone. One stamp was added.
COOLDOWN ACTIVEThe customer got a stamp too recently. They can get the next one after the cooldown.
DAILY CAP REACHEDThe customer has collected today's maximum. They can collect again tomorrow.
TAP THROTTLEDThe tag was tapped twice too quickly. Wait a few seconds and tap once.
INACTIVE NFC TAGThis tag has been switched off. Tell a manager, and give this customer a manual stamp.
SHOP PAUSEDStamping is paused for your shop. Contact your account manager.

Redeeming a reward

  1. When the customer has enough stamps, they tap the claim button on their card. A QR code appears with an 8-character check code (XXXX-XXXX) and a 5-minute timer.
  2. On the shop device, open Redeem Reward.
  3. Scan the QR into the Customer Redemption Token box, using one of these:
    • a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner (recommended), which types the code straight into the box; or
    • a QR-reader app on the shop device: scan, copy the text, and paste it into the box.
  4. Tap Verify Token & Check Balance.
  5. Check that the 8-character code on your screen matches the one on the customer's phone, and that it says ELIGIBLE FOR ….
  6. Confirm the redemption. The stamps are deducted and the code can't be used again. Serve the reward, then tap Scan Next Customer.
NoteThe 8-character code is for checking by eye only. You can't type it into the box instead of scanning the QR. If the code has expired, ask the customer to tap Refresh QR Code on their phone.

Instagram "follow us, size up" perk (if your shop offers it)

  1. The customer follows your shop on Instagram, then taps CLAIM UPSIZE.
  2. Scan and verify their code, the same way as a reward. The screen shows the free upsize perk.
  3. Ask the customer to open your Instagram profile on their phone. The button must read Following.
  4. Tap Follow Checked — Grant Free Upsize. No stamps are used. Each card can claim the upsize once. If you see UPSIZE ALREADY USED, it has already been claimed.

To turn this perk on, or change your Instagram handle, contact your account manager.

Manual stamp (when a phone can't tap)

  1. Open Manual Stamp.
  2. Enter the customer's full card ID. Their card only shows the first 8 characters (Card #XXXXXXXX), so a manager can find the full ID by matching those 8 characters in Activity & Audit or the Cards export.
  3. Enter a reason, for example "Phone NFC not working; receipt #4092". A reason is required and is recorded.
  4. Submit, and check for STAMP RECORDED — RESULT: STAMPED. The cooldown and daily cap still apply.
TipUse manual stamps only when you have to. Several manual stamps for the same customer raise a warning flag for your manager.

PrivacyThe programme follows the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Customers start anonymously: no name, phone number or email is needed. Anonymous visitors who never collect a stamp are deleted automatically after 30 days. Your exports contain anonymous card IDs only, never emails or phone numbers.
Tell customers to back upIf a customer hasn't backed up and clears their browser or loses their phone, their stamps can't be recovered. A quick "tap Back up to keep your stamps safe" at the counter prevents most complaints.

07Monitoring your shop

The system watches for unusual activity and highlights it for you. A few minutes a week is usually enough.

Activity & Audit (all roles)

Shows your shop's last 50 stamps (manual ones are labelled MANUAL), the last 50 redemptions and the last 50 recorded events. Anomaly Flags at the top highlight anything unusual, graded WARNING or HIGH:

FlagRaised whenWhat to do
Repeated Daily Cap / Cooldown ActivityA customer keeps hitting the daily cap or has several blocked tapsUsually harmless. Watch for staff tapping too eagerly.
High NFC Tag Volume / Rapid TapsOne tag has an unusually high number of stamps or rapid tapsCheck where the tag is kept. Rotate it if in doubt.
Frequent Manual Stamp OverridesOne customer received 3 or more manual stampsCheck the reasons recorded, and talk to the staff involved.

Tag-leak warning (Tags tab)

A yellow banner appears on the Tags tab when a tag looks as if its address has been shared: more than 8 different customers in 15 minutes, or more than 25 blocked rapid taps in an hour. Tap Rotate in the banner, then rewrite the physical tag.

Usage & cost (Plan & Billing)

Shows this month's active customers, the cost so far, whether your cap is protecting you, and any plan change due next month.

Data exports (Export tab, managers and owners)

Download Cards CSV gives one row per customer card (join date, branch, total and available stamps). Download Stamps CSV lists every stamp with its time, tag, branch and reason. Open them in Excel or Google Sheets to see busy hours, your best branches and how many customers come back.

08Troubleshooting & FAQ

The most common situations and how to handle them.

SituationWhat to do
"Access denied" when signing inYour access hasn't been activated yet, or has been switched off. Check you're using the invited email, then contact your owner or account manager.
I can't find the Rules, Brand or Tags screensGo to yoursite/staff/your-slug/settings and bookmark it. If you're sent back to sign-in, your role doesn't include that screen.
The magic link email didn't arriveCheck your spam folder, wait a minute, then request a new link. Each link works once.
The phone doesn't react to the tagAndroid: turn on NFC. iPhone: hold the tag at the top of the back. Phone cases thicker than about 3 mm can block NFC. If it still doesn't work, give a manual stamp.
The customer says a stamp is missingCheck Activity & Audit for their card number. Cooldown or daily-cap blocks are normal. If you find a real problem, contact support.
The reward QR has expiredAsk the customer to tap Refresh QR Code.
"Already claimed" on a rewardThat code has been used already, often because of a screenshot. Ask the customer to show the live code on their own card.
"Insufficient stamps"The customer doesn't have enough stamps for the reward yet.
A tag is lost or stolenTags tab → Rotate, then write and lock a new tag.
The customer changed phones and lost their cardIf they backed up, they use Recover with the same Google account or email. If they didn't, the stamps can't be recovered.
A staff member has leftOwner: Staff tab → Deactivate.
"Shop paused" on customer screensYour account is paused, so new stamps are stopped. Customers can still redeem rewards they've already earned for 30 days. Contact your account manager to resume.
I want a custom web address, a different timezone or currency, or the Instagram perkContact your account manager. These are set up for you.
